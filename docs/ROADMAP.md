# Roadmap

## Phase 0 — Discovery
- Baseline resolution time and top call drivers
- Map ERP/payment/fulfillment/logistics/invoice sources
- Define data classification and identity requirements

## Phase 1 — MVP
- Known-order lookup
- Payment status
- Pickup readiness
- Delivery tracking / ETA
- Invoice availability
- Order recovery with OTP
- Audit logging and escalation

## Phase 2 — Pilot
- Limited representative group
- Accuracy and tool-call quality measurement
- Agent usability and adoption feedback
- Failure/exception taxonomy

## Phase 3 — Production
- Enterprise SSO/RBAC
- Production carrier integrations
- Observability dashboards
- Incident and support runbooks
- Training and change-management rollout

## Phase 4 — Optimization
- Proactive delay notifications
- Next-best action
- Root-cause analytics
- Customer self-service with tighter identity controls
- Cost and latency optimization
